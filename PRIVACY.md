# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026

Coregulation Journal is designed as a private journaling app for caregivers. This policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

## Information You Enter

Coregulation Journal stores the information you choose to enter in the app, including:

- caregiver display name;
- child profile names and profile colors;
- behavior logs, including dates, intensity, duration, antecedents, behavior types, consequences, location, people present, outcomes, and notes;
- parent reaction logs, including emotional state, intensity, responses used, repair status, and notes;
- imported journal data from compatible JSON export files.

Do not enter information you do not want stored in the app or synced through your Apple iCloud account.

## How Your Information Is Stored

Journal data is stored on your device using Apple's local persistence frameworks. When iCloud is available, the app syncs your data through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services so your journal can be available on your Apple devices.

Coregulation Journal does not operate its own server for journal data.

## Sharing

The app may allow you to share selected child profile and behavior log data with another iCloud user using Apple's CloudKit sharing features. Sharing only happens when you choose to start a share through the app and Apple's sharing interface.

Parent reaction logs are intended to remain private to the caregiver who entered them.

## Export and Import

The app includes JSON export and import tools. If you export data, the exported file is controlled by you. Be careful where you save or send exported files because they may contain personal journal information.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Coregulation Journal does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

The app does not sell your data.

## Apple Services

Because the app uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit, Apple may process data as described in Apple's privacy policy and iCloud terms. Your use of iCloud is governed by your Apple account settings and Apple's policies.

## Support

If you contact support, you may choose to share information such as your email address, device details, screenshots, crash details, or a description of the issue. Support information is used only to respond to your request and improve the app.

## Children's Privacy

Coregulation Journal is intended for caregivers. It is not intended for children to use directly. Caregivers are responsible for deciding what information to enter about a child.

## Data Deletion

You can delete journal entries and child profiles in the app where deletion controls are available. You can also remove the app from your device and manage iCloud data through your Apple account and device settings.

Exported files are outside the app's control after you create or share them.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. When it changes, the effective date above will be updated.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the app's support channel listed on its App Store product page.
