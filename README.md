# Coregulation Journal Support

Public support, feedback, and policies for **Coregulation Journal**, an iOS app for caregivers who want to track behavior and co-regulation over time.

- **App Store:** [Coregulation Journal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coregulation-journal/id6762869597)
- **Support and feedback:** [open an issue](https://github.com/ntwkrgr/coregulation-journal-support/issues)
- **Privacy policy:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)

Current version: **1.2**

## About the App

Coregulation Journal helps caregivers record what happens before, during, and after a child's difficult moments, and how they themselves felt and responded. Over time, those logs show patterns that are hard to see day to day.

It is built around the ABC model (antecedent, behavior, consequence) and pairs each child's log with an optional reflection on the caregiver's own reaction, because regulation is something parents and children do together.

## Features

- **Behavior logs:** record the date, intensity, duration, antecedents, behavior types, consequences, location, people present, outcome, and notes. Review an entry before saving it.
- **Parent reaction logs:** record your own emotional state, intensity, the responses you used, and whether repair happened. Log a reaction on its own or linked to a behavior log.
- **Child profiles:** add a profile for each child with a name and color, plus optional birth month/year and gender.
- **History:** a day-by-day timeline of logs, and a per-child calendar where each day is colored by its highest intensity. Tap a day to see its logs.
- **Dashboard:** charts of child and parent trends over 7, 30, or 90 days. Swipe between the Child and My Trends views.
- **Insights (new in 1.2, optional):** short, on-device summaries written by Apple Intelligence. See [Insights](#insights).
- **iCloud sync:** your journal syncs across your Apple devices through your own iCloud account.
- **Co-parent sharing:** share a child's profile and behavior logs with another caregiver through iCloud. Your parent reaction logs stay private to you.
- **Export and import:** back up or move your data as a JSON file from **Settings → Data**.
- **No rating pop-ups:** the app never asks you for a review. If you'd like to leave one, there's a link in **Settings → About**.

## Insights

Insights is an optional feature introduced in version 1.2. It is off by default.

- **What it does:** Apple's on-device model reads your recent logs and writes one card per child and one for you. Each card covers patterns it found, a recommendation for each pattern, and a short closing note that highlights an improving trend or offers encouragement.
- **When cards appear:** a card unlocks after 3 logs. It uses the last 30 days of logs, and never fewer than the 10 most recent. Adding, editing, or deleting a log refreshes only the affected card.
- **Turning it on or off:** supported devices see a one-time introduction. You can also turn it on or off any time in **Settings → Apple Intelligence**.
- **Privacy:** everything runs on your device. Your logs and insights are never sent to us, to Apple's servers, or synced through iCloud. If you entered a child's birth month/year and gender, Insights uses the child's age and gender, never the birth date itself. Insights are deleted when you turn the feature off.
- **Limits:** Apple's safety filters sometimes decline a request. The app retries automatically and, if it still can't generate a card, shows a **Try again** button. Insights can quote text from your notes.

## Requirements

- iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
- An iCloud account for sync and co-parent sharing.
- Insights requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

## Privacy at a Glance

- No account with us, and no server of ours. Your journal lives on your device and in your own iCloud account.
- No ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.
- Your data is never sold.

Read the full [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

## Getting Help

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ntwkrgr/coregulation-journal-support/issues) to report a problem, ask a question, or suggest a feature. Including your iOS version, device model, and app version (1.2 or earlier) helps.

**Please don't include personal journal details, your child's information, or exported data files in an issue.** Issues on this repository are public.
